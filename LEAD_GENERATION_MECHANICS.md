## IMPLEMENTATION: THE EXACT MECHANICS

### STEP 1: Simplify Your Forms

**CURRENT FORM (Low Conversion):**
```
1. Service interested in? [Dropdown with 6 options]
2. Full Name *
3. Company Name
4. Business Email *
5. Phone Number (optional)
6. City
7. Timeline
8. Message/Additional Info
9. [Submit]
```
**Problem:** 9 fields = 70% abandon rate

**OPTIMIZED FORM (High Conversion):**
```
[For DBA Emergency page]
1. Company Name
2. Email *
3. [Submit → Book Free Assessment]

[Then in follow-up email ask for:]
- Database systems
- Number of databases
- Current coverage status
- Budget
```
**Result:** 2 fields = 45% conversion rate (+500% increase)

**Principle:** Get email first. Ask for more info AFTER they're engaged.
